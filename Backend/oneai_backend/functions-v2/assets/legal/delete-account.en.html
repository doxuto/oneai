Delete your One AI account

Doxuto Studio · Effective 24 September 2026

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open One AI and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is removed right away.

Without the app

Email contact@doxutostudio.top from the address you signed in with (Google or Apple). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Profile (email, name, photo)
  • All notes: audio, PDFs, transcripts, summaries, AI artifacts, chat history, tags, glossary
  • Share links (they stop working immediately)
  • Push notification tokens

What is kept

  • Purchase and subscription records held by Apple / Google / RevenueCat, as required for accounting. Cancel any active subscription in your App Store or Google Play settings — deleting the account does not cancel it.
  • Aggregated, anonymous usage statistics.
