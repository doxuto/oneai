Privacy Policy

One AI: AI Note Taker & Scribe · Doxuto Studio · Effective 24 September 2026

One AI ("the App") records or imports audio and documents, transcribes them, and produces AI notes, summaries, quizzes and answers. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and your choices. It applies to the iOS and Android apps and to the read-only share pages.

2. How AI processing works

  • Audio and text are sent to the providers above only to produce your transcript, notes and answers. Under our agreements with them, your content is not used to train their models.
  • Speaker labels, summaries and answers are generated automatically and may contain mistakes. Check important details against the recording.

3. How long we keep it

  • Original audio and PDF files: deleted automatically 7 days after processing on the free plan and 90 days on Premium. You can download the file before then; after that only the transcript and notes remain.
  • Transcripts, notes and chat: kept until you delete the note or your account.
  • Share links: live until you revoke them or delete the note.
  • Account deletion removes your profile, notes, audio, transcripts, chat history, glossary and device tokens immediately; backups are purged within 30 days. Purchase records stay with the app stores.

4. Ads and consent

The free plan shows ads (banner, interstitial and app-open formats). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent framework before any ad is loaded; on iOS we ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options. Premium removes ads. We do not offer rewarded ads and we never sell your personal data.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the processors listed above, under contracts that limit them to providing the service, and where the law requires. A note becomes visible to others only when you create a share link or export it.

6. Your rights

  • Export: share any note as PDF or text from the app.
  • Delete: remove a note, or your whole account, in Settings → Delete account (see also How to delete your account).
  • Access, correction, objection and portability requests: email contact@doxutostudio.top. We reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access to production data is limited to the operator and is logged. Share links use unguessable tokens and can be revoked.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, use the App with a parent's or guardian's permission.

9. International transfers

Our servers run in Google Cloud (Singapore region); AI providers may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and, for material changes, notify you in the App. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
